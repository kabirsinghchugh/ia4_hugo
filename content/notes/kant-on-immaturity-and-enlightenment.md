> *I now realize the weight of these words in a world which increasingly needs to remember*

- In 1784, Immanuel Kant described *humanity as being in a state of immaturity*, which to Kant is

> *“the inability to use one’s own understanding without the guidance of another”*

- > *(An Answer to the Question: ‘What is Enlightenment?’,*)
- For enlightenment of this kind, all that is needed is **Freedom**.
   - The freedom in question is the most innocuous form of all

> > *Freedom to make public use of one’s reason **in all matters***.
