Not all friction is bad. Some friction protects quality.

Examples:
- A checklist before publishing prevents avoidable mistakes.
- A waiting period before large purchases reduces impulse decisions.
- A review pass before shipping catches naming and clarity issues.

The rule: remove accidental friction, keep protective friction.
