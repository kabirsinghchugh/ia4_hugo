## Brian Eno & Ambient Music – Quick Guide

- **Ambient definition** – Eno coined the term; music should be _"as ignorable as it is interesting."_ It colours the environment without demanding focus.  
- **Foundational ambient albums**  
  - **Music for Airports** (1978) – the genre's prototype, serene loops.  
  - **On Land** (1982) – darker, field‑recording‑rich soundscapes, expanding the ambient palette.  
- **Art‑rock bridge** – **Another Green World** (1975) blends concise vocal tracks with instrumental pieces that pre‑figure his ambient style.  
- **Key collaborations**  
  - **My Life in the Bush of Ghosts** (1981, with David Byrne) – early sampling, world‑music fusion.  
  - **Fourth World Vol. 1: Possible Musics** (1980, with Jon Hassell) – "Fourth World" concept, melding tribal timbres with futuristic processing.  
- **Suggested playlist (one track each)**  
  1. _Music for Airports_ – "1/1"  
  2. _On Land_ – "Lizard Point"  
  3. _Another Green World_ – "The Big Ship"  
  4. _My Life in the Bush of Ghosts_ – "Regiment"  
  5. _Fourth World Vol. 1_ – "Chemistry"  
  6. _The Ship_ (2016) – "Fickle Sun (i)" – modern ambient evolution.
