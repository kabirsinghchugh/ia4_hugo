## Rice

### Ingredients

- 400 g rice
- 600 ml water (1.5 parts water to 1 part rice)
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Method

1. Sieve and rinse the rice thoroughly to remove excess starch.
2. Combine the rice and water in a pot. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce heat to low.
4. Cover with a lid and simmer for 8–10 minutes, giving a large stir occasionally.
