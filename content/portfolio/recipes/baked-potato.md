## Baked Potato

Use a glass bowl with a plate on top to hold the moisture. Medium-sized potatoes only — large ones don't cook evenly.

### Method

1. Scrub the potato clean.
2. Pierce the potato with a knife on each end.
3. Lather the potato with butter.
4. Microwave for 3–4 minutes.
5. Salt liberally.
6. Microwave for 2 more minutes (1 minute if your microwave is powerful).
7. Slice open the potato, add cheese, and microwave for 25 seconds to melt.
