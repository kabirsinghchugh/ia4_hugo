## White Sauce Pasta

This makes enough sauce for 455 g of penne.

### Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons butter
- 4 garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 tablespoons flour (see note below)
- 2 cups milk
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/2 cup parmesan, plus 1/4 cup for topping

### Method

1. On medium heat, melt the butter and soften the garlic for 1–2 minutes.
2. Add half the flour while stirring, then incorporate the rest. Stir constantly to prevent lumps.
3. Add the milk in the same gradual way — pour a little, stir until absorbed, repeat. This keeps the sauce smooth.
4. Add the cheese and stir until melted. Season with salt, pepper, oregano, and basil.
5. Toss with al dente pasta.
6. Plate and top with remaining parmesan and a handful of fresh parsley.

### Notes

- Use 2 tablespoons of flour rather than 3 — slightly less flour with a little extra milk gives a better texture.
- The gradual addition technique for both flour and milk is the key step. Rushing either causes a lumpy or pasty sauce.
