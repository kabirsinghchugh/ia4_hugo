## Hummus

This builds the tahini in two stages before combining with chickpeas.

### Method

1. Use overnight-soaked chickpeas. Heat them dry in a pan with 1 teaspoon baking soda.
2. Stir continuously for 3 minutes (dry stir — no water).
3. Add water and 1 teaspoon salt. Skim the foam and loose husks as they rise.
4. Cook for 20–30 minutes until the chickpeas are very soft, almost mush.
5. Drain and rinse with cold water, removing further husks. Make sure they are no longer hot before blending.
6. **Tahini stage 1 (T-1):** Dry-roast white sesame seeds in a pan on medium-low for 3–5 minutes until toasty. Process for 2 minutes, then add 3–4 tablespoons of oil and process for 2–3 more minutes. Add a pinch of salt.
7. **Tahini stage 2 (T-2):** Blend the juice of 2 lemons with one whole garlic clove and a splash of water.
8. Mix T-1 and T-2 together until the two runny components form a viscous paste.
9. Add small spoonfuls of water to the tahini paste until it reaches a smooth, thick-batter consistency.
10. Add the combined tahini paste to the chickpeas along with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt. Puree for about 2 minutes until smooth.
