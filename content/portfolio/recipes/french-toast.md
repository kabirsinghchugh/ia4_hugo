## French Toast

### Ingredients

- 2–3 eggs
- 100 ml milk
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 pinch cinnamon powder
- 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
- 1 teaspoon oil
- 1 teaspoon butter

### Method

1. Mix the eggs and milk together.
2. Add salt, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla essence. Stir to combine.
3. Preheat a pan over medium heat. Add the oil and butter.
4. When the butter starts to brown, dip a slice of bread in the egg mixture and place it in the pan.
5. Cook for about 1 minute, flip, and cook the other side until golden.
