## Lemonade

### Ingredients

- 1/4 cup hot water
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 3/4 cup cold water
- 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (about 1 large or 2 small lemons)
- Ice

### Method

Dissolve the sugar in the hot water. Add the cold water and lemon juice. Stir, add ice, and serve.
