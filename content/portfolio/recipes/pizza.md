## Pizza

### Ingredients

- 1 pizza base
- Mozzarella cheese, dethawed
- Pizza sauce
- 2 bell peppers, chopped
- 2 onions, chopped

### Method

#### Preparation

1. Chop the bell peppers and onions.
2. Make sure mozzarella is fully dethawed and ready.
3. Apply pizza sauce evenly to the base — not too much.
4. Add a first layer of cheese over the sauce.
5. Arrange the chopped bell peppers and onions over the cheese.
6. Add the remaining cheese on top.

#### Baking (General)

1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Place the baking tray at the bottom rack or one rack above the bottom; preheat the oven with the tray inside.
3. Line the preheated tray with baking paper, then place the assembled pizza on it.
4. Bake for 10 minutes at 180°C.

#### Baking (Morphy Richard OTG)

1. Preheat the OTG for 10 minutes at 180°C.
2. For best results, bake the pizza base alone first for 5 minutes at 180°C on the **BAKE** mode (bottom heating element only). This crisps the base before the toppings weigh it down.
3. Add all toppings to the pre-baked base.
4. Return to the oven and bake for a further 10–12 minutes at 180°C on BAKE mode.
