## Microwave Mashed Potatoes for Two

### Ingredients

- About 1 pound of potatoes, cut into 1-inch chunks (e.g., 4 rinsed, unpeeled Yukon Gold potatoes)
- 2 large pinches of salt
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (approximately 1/2 tablespoon per potato)
- 1 tablespoon half-and-half (or cream or milk)
- 2 teaspoons mayonnaise (optional)

### Method

1. Place the potato chunks in a microwave-safe bowl or dish.
2. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap, making a 1/2-inch slit in the top to vent.
3. Microwave for 9–12 minutes, or until the potatoes can be easily pierced with a fork. Cooking time will vary depending on the quantity of potatoes.
4. Remove the bowl from the microwave and roughly mash the potatoes with a large fork, wooden spoon, or potato masher — this lets excess moisture evaporate before the dairy goes in.
5. Add the butter and continue to mash until melted.
6. Stir in the half-and-half and mayonnaise (if using), and mash again until smooth and creamy.
7. Taste and season with additional salt as needed. Garnish as desired and serve immediately.

### Notes

- Even if you're mayo-averse, give it a try: it really helps emulsify the hydrophobic (fatty) with the hydrophilic (watery), which is why the texture comes out smoother than with butter and cream alone.
- The rough-mash step in step 4 is easy to skip but worth doing — potatoes hold a lot of steam moisture, and letting it escape first means a less watery result.
