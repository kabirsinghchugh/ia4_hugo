## Chicken Fry

**Serves:** 2–3 | **Cook time:** ~35 minutes

### Ingredients

- 500 g chicken pieces
- 3 tablespoons oil
- 2 teaspoons ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1.5 teaspoons coriander powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- Fresh coriander leaves, for garnish

### Method

1. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a frying pan over low heat. Add the ginger-garlic paste and fry for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
2. Add the chicken pieces. Increase heat to high and fry for about 2 minutes, until the chicken turns white on the outside.
3. Add turmeric and salt. Reduce heat to medium and fry for 2–3 minutes until the chicken begins to brown.
4. Reduce heat to low, cover with a lid, and cook for 10 minutes.
5. Flip and stir the chicken. Cover again and cook on low for another 10 minutes.
6. Add chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and garam masala. Increase to medium heat and fry uncovered for 2–3 minutes.
7. Add ketchup and continue to fry on medium for 3–4 minutes, stirring to coat.
8. Add fresh coriander leaves and fry for 1 more minute. Serve hot.
