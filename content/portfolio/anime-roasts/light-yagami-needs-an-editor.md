Light is what happens when a teenager confuses note-taking with sovereignty.
Every monologue arrives with the confidence of a final theorem and the practical
stability of wet cardboard.

## Premise

He treats secrecy, ego, and intellectual performance as if they are the same
skill. They are not. One of them is acting.

## Roast

The funniest part of Death Note is that Light keeps building elaborate
god-complex workflows when a normal person would simply stop leaving clues
everywhere.

## Card Logic

Short titles matter here. They make the inside collection page feel more like a
gallery of quick hits than a list of full essays.
