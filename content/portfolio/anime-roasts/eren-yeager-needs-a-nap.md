Eren's main hobby is turning one bad day into a civilization-level policy
mistake. The energy is committed, but the planning department is clearly on
strike.

This note format is simple on purpose: one character, one angle, one clean
punchline, and a small tag stack so the collection stays browsable.

## Premise

He behaves like every thought must be acted on before the second draft arrives.
That makes him compelling television and terrible institutional design.

## Roast

If your revolutionary worldview still sounds like "I am angry and therefore this
must be geopolitically correct," the problem is not destiny. The problem is
sleep.

## Why It Works

The best roast notes are compact enough to scan in a card grid, but specific
enough that the post still feels like a finished micro-essay.
