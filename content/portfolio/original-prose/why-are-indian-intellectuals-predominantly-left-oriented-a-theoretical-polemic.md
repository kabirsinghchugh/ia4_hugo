Indian society has been historically stratified into four Varnas, the Scholars(Brahmins), Nobility and Warriors(Kshatriyas), Merchants(Vaishyas), and the Workers(Shudras) and finally the Untouchables. Unlike other societies of Western Europe and China, where the nobility assumed the intellectual role alongside the church, in India, the Brahmins were the sole stakeholders of literature and knowledge, and had a social position uniquely above the aristocracy, despite the lack of means.

This social position entrenched the Brahmins to make norms to entrench their status, and over the millennia, even if access to the intellectual realm has been opened to people of all caste and class(another debate in its own right), the social status of the scholar remains as one of the elite positions in the society, or did so until the ascension of Capitalism, which threatened to dismantle this hierarchy, long since monopolised by the Left.
