# Spiritual Thought Experiments from Daoism

## Common Knowledge

>To know you, I must know _knowing_

>I can only know you from how I know myself

>To know myself I must know that the knowing of the knower is known, and it is known that it is known, and so on..

---

## Two Rights Make no Wrong

Aren’t words just vibrating air?

What does it mean to you when I utter - ‘love’

Is your love the same as mine? If not - then is the word meaningless?

How can we communicate, when my right is your wrong?

Isn’t then to right a wrong is to wrong a right?

---

## Who Serves Whom?

My organs-this brain, heart, lungs

Whom do they work for?

Does the brains serve the heart?

Does the heart beat for the brain?

Do we have brains so that we can fill our stomachs, or do we have stomachs to run our brain?

Are these organs servants? To whom?

Me? This I? Where does this I reside?

In one's brains? So do I stop existing if I lose my memories?

Is this I -- my body?

Or is the I in my body?

Our bodies deform. Cells replace each another. Who is then the master, for which the heart beats, even when you tell it not to.

Who is this master for whom we think, when we don't want to.

Who is this master that makes you think you control your breath, and laughingly takes back control from you, while you gasp

The master that this mind serves is the government, and all these rocks, plants, organs, cells, atoms -- all these are servants, all these are masters.

They are the government, the governor, and the governed.

## Life’s A Race

Racing ourselves to other exhaustion

During the race, it all happens.

Do we focus on racing yet we stop on the track to think of it?

Technically we're still racing, but what's the point if this heartless race just involves where is some labour.

What happens after the race? Or their prices? Do we get to rest at home for a hot bath or do we treat or injuries or are scolded by or coach? Or are we embarrassed by the crowd?
