## Political Isekai Plot

_On an ordinary school day, a whole school, including students and teachers, are transported into a parallel world through a rift opened by a mysterious cosmic event. This new world, called **Evandis**, is a mix of fantasy, magic, and ancient civilizations ._

As the people try to adjust, they find themselves in a "state of nature," with no existing government or structure. Inspired by various political philosophies they learned from school, the students and teachers form various factions, each adopting different ideologies. These factions serve as the various houses in this political game, with the following ideologies:

1. **Locke's Laborers:** Believers in the right to property and civil society, emphasizing the role of mutual agreement among citizens.
2. **Hobbes's Leviathans:** Advocates for a powerful, central authority to prevent society from falling into chaos.
3. **Marx's Proletariat:** A group of students who are adamant that a classless society is the way to equality, with resources shared collectively.
4. **Rousseau's Social Contract:** Followers of a communal lifestyle, where people can retain their freedom while still contributing to the society.
5. **Machiavellian Monarchs:** Followers of realism, they believe in the efficient execution of power, doing whatever it takes to achieve their ends.
6. **Nietzschean Übermenschen:** Those who seek the creation of new values in this seemingly meaningless world, through the power of the human will.
7. **Stirner's Egoists:** They prioritize individual self-interest and the pursuit of autonomy above all societal constraints and morality.

Our protagonist, Kazuki, a history and philosophy-loving student, forms a group based on Nozick's minarchist society philosophy - the **Nozick's Nightwatchmen**. They strive to establish a society with the minimum level of governance necessary, focusing on individual rights and a free-market system.

The primary antagonists are the **Rawlsians**, led by the charismatic and seemingly noble Ryouta. They believe in the "Theory of Justice," aiming to create a society where everyone benefits, starting from the most disadvantaged. Their motives seem noble at first glance, but they have an ulterior motive of creating an equal society at any cost, potentially leading to forced redistributions that unsettle other factions.

The plot evolves into a complex political drama, similar to Game of Thrones, as each faction tries to implement their political philosophy in their respective territories and extend their influence. Some groups prosper, others diminish and get absorbed, and alliances are forged and broken. The main conflict emerges between the Nozick's Nightwatchmen and the Rawlsians, as Kazuki and Ryouta's different interpretations of justice clash.

"State of Parallel: Isekai Senkyo" follows these groups' evolution, survival, alliances, and battles, focusing on the strengths and weaknesses of their chosen ideologies. It delves into what it means to create a new society, the struggles of maintaining order, and how differing philosophies can clash in the most dramatic of ways. It is an exploration of political philosophy within the framework of an action-packed, emotionally charged, and thought-provoking anime.

**Chapter 1: The Scheming Stage**

The moon hung low over the vast landscape of Evandis, casting a spectral glow on the rugged terrain below. Amidst this tranquility, a fortress carved into the mountainside thrummed with tension. This was the stronghold of the Nozick's Nightwatchmen, a beacon of minimum governance in a world of chaotic ideologies.

In the command room, Kazuki, their leader, was hunched over a parchment spread across the table. His eyes flickered with the candlelight, scrutinizing the intricate sketches and scribbled notes that represented the current political landscape. His heart pounded with the weight of the decisions he was about to make.

His thoughts were interrupted by the soft creak of the door. Akiyama, his trusted aide, walked in, a sheaf of parchment under his arm. He spread them across the table, revealing scout reports of Rawlsian movements along their eastern border.

"Ryouta is on the move, Kazuki. He's targeting the weaker factions, the Stirner's Egoists, and Rousseau's Social Contract, promising them equality and justice," Akiyama said, his face grave.

Kazuki clenched his fist, his mind racing. A direct conflict with the Rawlsians could lead to a disastrous war. He had to negotiate, form alliances, and outwit Ryouta. His thoughts shifted to the various factions, their strengths, their weaknesses. The game had begun, and he was in the heart of it.

---

**Chapter 2: The Rift**

Only six months prior, none of this existed. Kazuki was an average high school student in Japan, more interested in debating political philosophies than in sports or parties. All that changed when his school was engulfed by a mysterious, pulsating rift.

He remembered the sudden jolt, the flashes of cosmic energy, and the gut-wrenching feeling of being ripped away from their world. When they woke up, they were in Evandis, a breathtaking world of towering mountains, vast forests, and a sky painted with the hues of an alien world.

As the students and teachers struggled to comprehend their situation, it became clear that they were in a "state of nature." With no existing structure, the school had to become a microcosm of society. Inspired by their school lessons, they started to form factions, each representing a different political philosophy.

Kazuki found himself drawn to the ideology of Nozick's minarchist society. With a group of like-minded students, he formed the Nozick's Nightwatchmen, advocating for a society with the minimum level of governance, with a focus on individual rights and a free-market system.

The real game began when Ryouta rose to power, with his theory of justice and equal society, thus shaping the world of Evandis into a chessboard of political ideologies. The echoes of their first meeting still rang in Kazuki's mind as he reflected on the present turmoil.

What started as an alienated school in an unknown world had now evolved into a ground of political struggle and conflict, and this was just the beginning.
