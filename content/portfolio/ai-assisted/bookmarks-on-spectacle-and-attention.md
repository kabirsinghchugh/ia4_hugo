One way to use this collection is to save fragments that sharpen your own
language. The excerpt is the object; the short framing note is the reason to
keep it.

## Excerpt

When platforms optimize for spectacle, they also train attention toward speed,
novelty, and emotional compression. That changes what feels worth reading.

## Why Save It

I want a place for quotations that deserve one paragraph of commentary, not a
full essay and not just a dead bookmark.

## Excerpt

An interface becomes legible when the user can predict the next move without
having to rehearse the whole system in their head.

## Why Save It

This is the kind of line that belongs in a collection page rather than a loose
notes inbox. It is short, reusable, and easy to revisit later.
